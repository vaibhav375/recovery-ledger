# Claims registry

Every claim this project pre-registered, the rule fixed before the run,
and what the evidence said. Generated by `make claims` from the
artifacts themselves — nothing here is written by hand, so a claim
cannot be recorded as holding by anyone's decision.

- **N2.dnd-signal**: The supporting signal for N2. Published at 1.29x after the original 1.93x was found to be a single small-sample draw.
- **calibration.monotone**: Fails at 0.879 on one draw. Reported as failed rather than rounded up; near-monotone is tracked as the weaker claim.
- **regret.cost-disagreement**: Outside the interval in 3 of 6 draws. Unresolved, and published as unresolved — the limitation itself did not replicate.
- **dr.cross-fitting**: Refuted: coverage and mean gap are flat across a 10x fold range.
- **tier1b.real-money-effect**: The only money figure in this repo not produced by its own generator: $424 per 1,000 customers, interval excluding zero.
- **tier1b.targeting-on-real-money**: Not established. Three estimators agree on the sign - all the registered rule asked for - but the paired interval on the difference covers zero. The rule was weaker than the question and is left as registered, with the interval reported beside it.
- **lambda.dominance**: The project's worst near-miss. Published as dominance from a run that no longer reproduced; it trades, it does not dominate.
